Discover Your Tribe with TribeFind

TribeFind is the social discovery app that connects you with like-minded people based on shared interests and real-time location. Whether you're looking for workout buddies, foodie friends, or adventure companions, TribeFind helps you find your tribe effortlessly.

<b>KEY FEATURES:</b>

<b>Smart Location Matching</b>
• Find people with similar interests nearby
• Real-time location sharing with privacy controls
• Discover local events and activities

<b>Interest-Based Connections</b>
• Connect through 100+ shared hobbies and activities
• Join interest-based communities
• Participate in group activities and meetups

<b>Seamless Communication</b>
• In-app messaging with your connections
• Group chats for shared activities
• Real-time notifications for new matches and messages

<b>Visual Storytelling</b>
• Share moments with your tribe through Stories
• Photo and video capture with filters
• Stories auto-expire after 24 hours

<b>Privacy & Safety First</b>
• Complete control over location sharing
• Granular privacy settings for all interactions
• Safe and secure authentication

<b>Social Integration</b>
• Sign in with Google, Apple, or Twitter
• Link your existing social accounts
• Expand your network seamlessly

Whether you're new to a city, looking to expand your social circle, or want to find people who share your passions, TribeFind makes meaningful connections effortless and fun.
